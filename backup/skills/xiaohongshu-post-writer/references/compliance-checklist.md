# Compliance checklist

Run this check on the final draft and attached media.

## Truth

- Are visits, purchases, usage, prices, dates, durations, and results supported?
- Does “我” refer to the user's real experience?
- Are statistics, rankings, and “latest” claims sourced and current?
- Does the title deliver what the body and images contain?

## Persona

- Does the tone match the supplied profile?
- Are only relevant identity anchors used?
- Is expertise calibrated correctly?
- Has click optimization distorted the long-term account image?

## Privacy and confidentiality

Check media and text for:

- QR codes, barcodes, serial numbers, IDs, tickets, addresses
- license plates, faces of private people, minors, reflections
- employee numbers, badges, workstations, screens, internal tools
- client names, project data, unreleased products, confidential metrics
- precise home or routine location

Recommend crop, blur, omission, or more general wording where appropriate.

## Commercial and regulated claims

- Identify sponsorship, gifted products, affiliate relationships, or sales intent.
- Remove unsupported efficacy, medical, financial, legal, safety, or income claims.
- Remove absolute claims such as “最好、第一、百分百、永久、零风险”.
- Distinguish observable experience from causal claims.
- Add required disclosures rather than disguising promotion as an independent review.

## Community quality

- No requests to like, save, follow, or exchange engagement.
- No unrelated trending tags or deceptive cover text.
- No copied passages, fake testimonials, or simulated comments.
- No demeaning, discriminatory, sexualized, violent, or harassing framing.
- No off-platform diversion that violates current platform rules.

## AI use

- Is any image, text, audio, or video substantially AI-generated or synthesized?
- Could a viewer reasonably mistake synthetic material for a real person, place, event, or result?
- Should the platform's AI-content label be recommended?
- Is the content part of batch automation or simulated-human account operation?

Return a short result:

```text
合规检查：通过 / 修改后通过 / 需确认
已修正：
- ...
发布前确认：
- ...
AI标识建议：建议标识 / 通常无需标识 / 依平台发布入口确认
```
