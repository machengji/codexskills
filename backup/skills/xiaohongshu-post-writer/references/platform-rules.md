# Xiaohongshu platform rules

Last verified: 2026-07-27.

Treat this as a working summary, not a substitute for current platform notices or legal advice. Browse current authoritative sources when the user asks for the latest rule, when publishing regulated content, or when this verification date is stale.

## Stable principles

- Keep titles, covers, body text, and images materially consistent.
- Do not fabricate experiences, reviews, identities, transactions, or engagement.
- Do not request or trade likes, saves, follows, or other fake interaction.
- Avoid spam, repetitive low-value templates, keyword stuffing, and misleading trend-jacking.
- Disclose commercial relationships and follow applicable advertising rules.
- Avoid absolute or guaranteed claims, especially for products and services.
- Protect copyright, likeness, privacy, confidential information, and minors.
- Apply stricter checks to medical, health, finance, legal, education, beauty efficacy, and other regulated claims.

## AI-assisted content

Publicly reported Xiaohongshu governance positions in 2026:

- The platform encourages responsible AI-assisted creation.
- Creators should truthfully label AI-generated or synthesized content.
- The platform may add a label when it identifies unlabeled AI content.
- AI-enabled fabrication, infringement, misleading content, account farming, automated impersonation, and fake interaction can lead to distribution limits or account penalties.
- AI-managed accounts that automatically generate, post, and simulate human interaction are a distinct high-risk category.

Using AI to organize a user's real experience is not the same as fabricating a life or running an autonomous account. When the text, image, audio, or video is substantially generated or synthesized, recommend using the platform's available AI-content label. Do not promise that labeling guarantees distribution.

## Claims to avoid presenting as official

- A fixed CES formula assigning exact weights to likes, saves, comments, shares, or follows
- Guaranteed traffic-pool thresholds
- Guaranteed shadow-ban tests
- Claims that all AI-assisted text is automatically suppressed
- Claims that “去AI味” can or should evade platform detection

State uncertainty and separate platform-confirmed rules from creator folklore.
