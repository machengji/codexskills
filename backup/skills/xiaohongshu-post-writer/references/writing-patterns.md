# Writing patterns

Choose patterns by reader need, not by copying surface-level “爆款” language.

## Title construction

Build titles from two or three components:

- audience or situation
- concrete tension/problem
- verified result or payoff
- low-cost format
- place/time when search-relevant

Useful structures:

```text
[人群/场景]｜[具体问题]后，我留下了[数字]个方法
[地点/时间]＋[真实经历]：最意外的是[具体细节]
[痛点]？我实际会先做这[数字]步
[任务]怎么选｜按[维度]一张图看懂
[反差事实]，但真正让我记住的是[细节]
```

Avoid:

- guarantees: “必火”“百分百有效”
- unverifiable superlatives: “全网第一”“最好”
- bait that the body does not deliver
- a string of exaggerated adjectives

## Opening patterns

Open with one of:

- the most concrete fact
- a small surprise or contrast
- the user's actual question
- a scene visible in the first image
- a concise judgment earned by experience

Do not open with a generic industry history or “在这个快节奏的时代”.

## Body patterns

### Story / daily life

```text
specific scene
→ what happened
→ one or two memorable details
→ personal reaction
→ restrained reflection
```

### Guide / collection

```text
who this is for
→ selection criteria
→ item / step / route
→ benefit and limitation
→ quick recap
```

### Experience / career

```text
personal scope disclaimer
→ concrete change or problem
→ what the author tried or noticed
→ current judgment
→ what remains uncertain
```

### Product or service experience

```text
context and need
→ what was actually used
→ observable result
→ drawbacks or fit boundaries
→ disclosure and recommendation scope
```

Never manufacture a purchase, visit, test, or result.

## Interaction by goal

- **Views:** make the value legible before adding mystery.
- **Saves:** include reusable facts, steps, prices, routes, or comparisons.
- **Comments:** ask a narrow question answerable from experience.
- **Search:** use natural task, audience, product, and location phrases.
- **Trust:** show evidence, limits, and personal scope.

Avoid direct requests for likes, saves, follows, or comment quotas.

## Reduce “AI味”

- Anchor each paragraph in a supplied fact, image, action, or opinion.
- Prefer one strong detail over three generic adjectives.
- Remove symmetrical “不是…而是…” chains when overused.
- Avoid ending every paragraph with a lesson.
- Keep occasional imperfection or uncertainty when truthful.
- Do not insert slang that conflicts with the persona.
- Use emoji as punctuation accents, not structural scaffolding.

## Tags

Use a small set covering:

1. core topic
2. audience or use case
3. place or category when relevant
4. account pillar

Avoid irrelevant trending tags and repeated synonyms.
